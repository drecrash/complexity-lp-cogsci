## Background

I recently got very interested in the sciences of complexity, optimization, and computational cognition, so I made a rough six-week long "curriculum" for me to follow and get up to speed on these topics before I start my first semester at UCSB.

---

## Plan

**Complexity and Linear Programming**

I've chosen textbooks for studying complex systems and linear optimization.

Each week, I will complete some number chapters or sections from each textbook, take notes, and work through their exercises. More importantly, I will also be implementing 1 - 2 programming projects based on the material from either or both textbooks.

The point is less to get better at programming, and more to just get practice applying the concepts from each chapter.

**Cognitive Science**

For cognitive science (CS), I'm taking a less structured approach. Every week, I'll try to read, annotate, and summarize 3-7 research papers or academic articles on CS principles and sub-fields. <br>
